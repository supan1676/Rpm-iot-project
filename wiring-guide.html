⚡ System Architecture — Complete Wearable

ℹ️
MLX90614 Clock & Power Note
3.3V SMBus device

Standard MLX90614 breakout boards run reliably from the ESP32-S3's 3.3V rail with built-in pull-up resistors.

I2C Speed Note: The MLX90614 uses SMBus timing specifications and operates most reliably with an I2C clock speed of 50 kHz.

🔥
Vibration Motor Direct GPIO
Will burn your ESP32-S3

A coin vibration motor draws 60–100mA at startup. ESP32-S3 GPIO pins are rated for maximum 40mA (recommended ≤20mA).

Additionally, DC motors generate inductive kickback — voltage spikes when the motor turns off — that can destroy the GPIO pin.

Fix: Use a 2N7000 N-channel MOSFET (or 2N2222 NPN transistor) to switch the motor, with a 1N4148 flyback diode across the motor terminals. Or buy a "vibration motor module" that includes the driver circuit on the board.

🖐️
MAX30102 Wrist Placement
Won't work reliably on bare wrist

The MAX30102 uses Red (660nm) + IR (880nm) LEDs. These wavelengths need thin tissue with dense capillaries (fingertip, earlobe).

On the wrist, thick skin + fat + tendons + bone scatter the signal. Commercial smartwatches use green LEDs (~530nm) with custom optics and heavy DSP — technology the bare module doesn't have.

Fix: Mount the MAX30102 as a finger-clip probe connected via a short cable to the wrist unit. This gives clinical-grade readings with minimal code complexity.

🤚
Wrist Fall Detection False Alarms
Arm swings trigger >2.5G spikes

On a belt/waist, a simple >2.5G threshold reliably detects falls. On the wrist, clapping, slamming a hand on a table, or swinging your arm quickly all exceed 2.5G.

Fix: Implement 2-stage verification in code:

1. Impact spike >3.0G on accelerometer
2. Followed by 5 seconds of near-zero angular velocity on gyroscope (confirming the patient is immobile, not just gesturing)

🌡️
Skin vs Core Temperature
Wrist reads 3°C lower than expected

Wrist skin temperature is typically 31–34°C, not the 37°C core body temperature patients and doctors expect.

Fix: Add a static calibration offset in your code: coreTempEstimate = skinTemp + 3.0; — and clearly label it as "Estimated Core Temp" on the display/telemetry.

📌
MPU6050 AD0 Pin
Intermittent I2C failures if left floating

The AD0 pin determines the I2C address (LOW = 0x68, HIGH = 0x69). If left unconnected (floating), it picks up electrical noise and randomly switches between addresses.

Result: MPU6050 works sometimes, fails randomly. Hard to debug because it seems intermittent.

Fix: Always tie AD0 to GND with a wire. This costs nothing and permanently fixes the address to 0x68.
